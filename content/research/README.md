# Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, spatial data analysis, and data engineering**, with a particular focus on extracting structured, meaningful information from real-world geographic data.

I am especially interested in problems where machine learning methods meet complex data pipelines — from data acquisition and preprocessing to modeling, evaluation, and practical deployment. Spatial data, with its inherent structure, scale, and uncertainty, provides a rich and challenging domain for this type of research.

## Current Position

I am a **PhD student in the discipline of Technical Computer Science and Telecommunications** and a **research and teaching assistant at the Department of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (Katedra Informatyki i Sztucznej Inteligencji)**, Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Automatics, Computer Science and Biomedical Engineering (WEAIiIB) at AGH University of Kraków. My doctoral and teaching work build on my earlier research in machine learning, geospatial data analysis, and formal modelling.

## Master’s Thesis

My Master’s thesis, **“Prediction of Topographic Object Characteristics through Integration of Spatial Data from Multiple Sources”** (*Predykcja charakterystyk obiektów topograficznych poprzez integrację danych przestrzennych z wielu źródeł*), was carried out under the supervision of **Sebastian Ernst, PhD**. In this work I developed a system for detecting and geolocalizing vertical road signs from dashcam video sequences, together with a spatial data fusion pipeline that automates inference and assigns priority attributes to the road graph. The thesis provided the foundation for the paper *“Modelling and Logical Verification of Road Priority in Poland.”*

## Bachelor’s Thesis and Conference Work

My Bachelor’s thesis, **“OSM data augmentation using ML methods,”** focused on developing a system for automatically enriching OpenStreetMap data using aerial imagery and machine learning techniques. This work was conducted under the supervision of **Sebastian Ernst, PhD**, at AGH University of Kraków.

The results of this research were presented at the **6th Polish Conference on Artificial Intelligence (PP-RAI 2025)** as an oral presentation and a poster — an invaluable experience that allowed me to engage with the broader AI research community and receive feedback from fellow researchers.

## Research Themes

My current and ongoing research interests include:
- machine learning for spatial and geospatial data,
- data engineering pipelines for large-scale, heterogeneous datasets,
- remote sensing and aerial imagery analysis,
- formal modelling and logical verification of road networks,
- practical applications of deep learning in real-world systems,
- bridging theoretical methods with robust engineering solutions.
